# Version notes

## Version 3 — manuscript dated October 23, 2026

**Critical Jumps for Rough Polynomial Carleson Operators with Angular Hardy Data and Finite Radial Variation**

- Strong critical-jump estimates for mean-zero angular Hardy data, with the polynomial phase and jump-amplitude suprema both taken pointwise before the spatial norm.
- A coefficient-uniform fixed-phase weak type (1,1) endpoint under the mean-zero angular L log L hypothesis.
- Every fixed finite radial variation exponent in both estimates, with complete sharp-truncation trajectories and their maximal, r-variation (r > 2), and principal-value consequences.
- A reorganized exposition, endpoint counterexamples, and precise literature comparisons, including Ding–Liu's radial-phase result and Chen–Ji's weak estimates.
- An updated title, abstract, bibliography and manuscript citation.
